Lochranza

Wednesday's welcome round, over the road from the distillery. An 11-hole par-3 pitch and putt laid out around the Chalmadale Burn, with ponds, bridges, its own standing stone, and red deer (and the odd hill sheep) wandering across it.

Heads up. The ST27 deck lists Lochranza as a 9-hole, 2,332-yard course. That was the old layout. It's now an 11-hole par-3 pitch and putt, so leave the driver in the bag.

ClubsThe campsite gives you a pitching iron, putter and balls at reception, included in the fee.
PayingThe Arran Golf Pass is accepted. No booking needed.
Hours8am to dusk, seven days a week, until the end of October.
DeerRed deer roam the course. Give them room, and don't count on them moving.

Clifftop links with seven par 3s and a string of blind shots. Local knowledge beats length here, so walk it or read this first.

TeesVisitors play the yellow tees. Visitors always play 12, never 18. A second loop is a separate booking.
SignalsOn blind holes, use the lever, pulley or bell at the green once you're clear so the group behind can play (confirmed on the 7th).
Yardage discsRed 100 · yellow 150 · white 200, measured to the middle of the green.
BurnsTwo burns are in play on the 2nd, 7th and 9th. Short on 2, long on 7, lay up on 9.

Lamlash

Hilly heathland above Lamlash Bay: 11 par 3s, steep slopes, blind tee shots to marker poles and burns in odd places. It climbs for the first 12 holes and comes back down to finish.

LegsHoles 1 to 12 are uphill work. Buggies are worth it for anyone who doesn't fancy the climb.
LiesExpect uphill and downhill lies all day. Swing within yourself and take an extra club uphill.
GreensRaised and quick. Short and below the hole is the safe miss on most holes.
MarkersBlind tee shots on 6 and 8, and a carry on 15: aim at the poles and commit.

18 holes · par 63 white / 62 yellow · 4,451 / 4,092 yds · hillside · Saturday 09:00

Whiting Bay

A hill course above the village, founded 1895: a stiff climb over the first four holes, then views across the Firth of Clyde to Holy Isle and Ben Lomond. Ten par 3s, blind shots, bells and fast, tricky greens. Saturday morning, before the final at Shiskine.

BellsThe 7th and 14th are blind from the tee. Wait for the bell before you drive, and ring it when you've putted out.
MarkersMarker posts show the line on the 1st, 8th, 9th, 10th, 12th and 14th. Trust them.
TeesThe 9th is a par 4 off the whites but a par 3 off the yellows. Agree tees before the match starts.
The climbHoles 1 to 4 climb steadily. Carry bags make it easier.

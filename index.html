The Team Code

A happy team is a winning team. Seven promises we make to each other.

  1. Every half point is celebratedHalves win Ryder Cups. We cheer them like wins.
  2. Nobody walks off aloneAfter a lost match, a teammate is waiting on the 18th.
  3. No blame, everBad holes happen. We move on to the next tee together.
  4. Back your partnerWhoever we're paired with, trust builds hole by hole.
  5. Duty nights and our nightEarly dinners on playing nights. The big night comes after the Ryder, and it's a good one.
  6. Know the courseWe walk or study Shiskine's blind shots together before we play it.
  7. Green on the first teeTeam kit on, team photo, then we go.

Shiskine

Clifftop links with seven par 3s and a string of blind shots. Local knowledge beats length here, so walk it or read this first.

TeesVisitors play the yellow tees. Visitors always play 12, never 18. A second loop is a separate booking.
SignalsOn blind holes, use the lever, pulley or bell at the green once you're clear so the group behind can play (confirmed on the 7th).
Yardage discsRed 100 · yellow 150 · white 200, measured to the middle of the green.
BurnsTwo burns are in play on the 2nd, 7th and 9th. Short on 2, long on 7, lay up on 9.

18 holes · par 64 · 4,510 yds white / 3,945 yellow · heathland

Lamlash

Hilly heathland above Lamlash Bay: 11 par 3s, steep slopes, blind tee shots to marker poles and burns in odd places. It climbs for the first 12 holes and comes back down to finish.

LegsHoles 1 to 12 are uphill work. Buggies are worth it for anyone who doesn't fancy the climb.
LiesExpect uphill and downhill lies all day. Swing within yourself and take an extra club uphill.
GreensRaised and quick. Short and below the hole is the safe miss on most holes.
MarkersBlind tee shots on 6 and 8, and a carry on 15: aim at the poles and commit.

Brodick

Part parkland, part along the shore, with the Rosa Burn in play and views of Goat Fell. Eight par 3s, two of them over the water, and a 220-yard par 3 to finish.

Local knowledge wanted. Brodick doesn't publish a hole-by-hole guide, so most holes below show the scorecard only. Whoever plays it first, add what you find on each hole.

TeesThe 2nd is a par 4 off the whites but a par 3 off the yellows. Agree tees before the match starts.
WaterTwo par 3s carry the Rosa Burn, including the 15th by the bridge. Take enough club.
TreesTree-lined in places, with a big Scots pine in the middle of the 5th fairway.
WindWind makes a big difference here. Check it on every tee.
